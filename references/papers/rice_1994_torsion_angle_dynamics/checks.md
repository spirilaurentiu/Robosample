# Checks / fixtures — Rice & Brünger 1994, Torsion Angle Dynamics

These are the concrete numbers stated in the paper. Most are empirical
crystallographic-refinement outcomes on one specific test system, so they are
protocol-level regression anchors rather than deterministic unit tests. Use them
to sanity-check a torsion-angle-MD refinement pipeline reproduction; do not
expect bit-exact reproduction (stochastic initial velocities, 10 repeats).

## Test system

- Protein: alpha-amylase inhibitor **1HOE-467A**, 74 amino acids.
- Program: extended X-PLOR; force field **PARHCSDX** (Engh & Huber).
- Diffraction data: 70% complete for $|F_{\text{obs}}| > 2\sigma$.
- MIR mean figure of merit = **0.69** (5-3 Angstrom); max ~0.82 (low-res), min ~0.57 (high-res).
- Resolutions: high = 5-2 Angstrom; medium = 5-3 Angstrom.
- Initial (good) model: backbone rms = **0.4 Angstrom**, non-H rms = **0.8 Angstrom**.

## Radius of convergence (headline results)

- Given slow-cooling simulated annealing at 5-2 Angstrom: converges only if
  backbone rms deviation < **1.25 Angstrom** (equivalently mean phase error up to
  ~80 degrees, ~1.3 Angstrom backbone rms).
- Given high-temperature (10,000 K) torsion angle dynamics + repeated
  refinements: corrects backbone rms deviations up to **~1.7 Angstrom**.

## Three scrambled test models (5-2 Angstrom, $\Delta\varphi_{\text{calc}}$ / backbone rms)

| model | $\Delta\varphi_{\text{calc}}$ (deg) | backbone rms (Angstrom) |
|---|---|---|
| least scrambled | 79 | 1.25 |
| medium scrambled | 82 | 1.45 |
| most scrambled | 84 | 1.63 |

## Convergence behavior (5-2 Angstrom, 10 repeats each)

- Least scrambled: every refinement (all protocols) converges.
- Most scrambled: neither conventional nor torsion-angle dynamics converge at
  5000 K; some torsion-angle refinements converge at 10,000 K.
- Best-4 averaged structure factors of most-scrambled model: $\Delta\varphi_{\text{calc}}$
  = **41.8 degrees** (vs. average individual 44 degrees); the four best have mean
  phase errors 41.7, 41.8, 43.2, 49.2 degrees.

## Reference-quality lower bounds (this system, 2 Angstrom)

- Slow-cool starting from the crystal structure: phase error **26 degrees**,
  backbone rms 0.17 Angstrom, non-H rms 0.40 Angstrom.
- 200-step conjugate-gradient minimization of crystal structure: phase error
  **15.5 degrees**, backbone rms 0.14 Angstrom, non-H rms 0.18 Angstrom.
- All converged refinements have $\Delta\varphi_{\text{calc}} > 30$ degrees.

## Medium resolution (5-3 Angstrom, 10 repeats)

- Phase restraints [Eq. 4], least scrambled: all 10 5000 K refinements converge
  (conv. and torsion), convergence = final backbone rms < 1 Angstrom.
- Phase restraints, medium scrambled: only 2 conventional 5000 K converge; 0
  torsion 5000 K; 4 torsion 10,000 K converge.
- Vector residual [Eq. 5], medium scrambled: 6/10 torsion dynamics, 5/10
  conventional converge.
- Vector residual, most scrambled: 1/10 conventional 5000 K vs. 5/10 torsion
  10,000 K converge.
- Even worse model (initial backbone rms 1.83 Angstrom): 4 torsion 10,000 K
  converge, 0 conventional 5000 K.

## Timings (Table I, one refinement, HP 735)

| refinement type | avg time (s) |
|---|---|
| conventional slowcool | 4627 |
| 5,000 K conventional dynamics | 5186 |
| 5,000 K torsion angle dynamics | 3546 |
| 10,000 K torsion angle dynamics | 4058 |

- Given identical 4 ps 5000 K stage: torsion angle refinement is **~20% faster**
  than conventional (5186 -> 3546 s from Table I). Raising torsion to 10,000 K
  adds only modest cost (3546 -> 4058 s).

## Integration / protocol parameters (implementation fixtures)

- Timestep: **0.002 ps** for torsion angle dynamics; **0.0005 ps** for conventional
  Cartesian dynamics.
- Constant-temperature stage: 4 ps at 5000 K or 10,000 K (some runs 8 ps).
- Quench: 0.1 ps conventional MD at 300 K.
- Minimization: 100 CG steps pre-dynamics (against $E_{\text{chem}}$, $C^{\alpha}$
  harmonically restrained), 120 CG steps post.
- Slowcool schedule (Table I footnote): start 3000 K, decrease 25 K every 0.025
  ps until 300 K -> 2.7 ps trajectory.
- Scrambling generated by 600 K MD without X-ray data.
- Integrator: 4th-order Runge-Kutta for torsion angle dynamics.
- Base body inversion: only a single $6\times6$ matrix inversion per step at the
  root (recursion cost is O(N) in bodies).
