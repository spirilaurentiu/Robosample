3// ============================================================================
4// World - reimplemented, SimTK-free. Each World owns ONE robot (immutable
5// RobotModel + live RobotState), a RobotEngine (stateless ops), and a
6// ForceBridge to OpenMM.
7//
8// Regimes (selected by moveType / cartesian_):
9// Cartesian world : plain OpenMM MD on the device (integrateTrajectoryOnDevice).
10// Torsional/robotic: internal-coordinate Generalized-Coordinate HMC. Sampling
11// = seed u via sqrt(M^-1), Verlet stepTo, Metropolis on the
12// FULL Hamiltonian H = PE + KE + Fixman - 1/2 RT logSineSqr.
13// Docking world : a torsional world whose ligand molecule roots are Free
14// and everything else is Welded/Rigid. Its move is a rigid
15// KICK of the ligand (a symmetric Cartesian proposal,
16// Metropolised on dU), optionally followed by the standard
17// MD-HMC relaxation sub-move. Root mobility here is a
18// *World* property, supplied by Context::addDockingWorld.
19// ============================================================================
