Detailed Description

Deterministic BAT (bond/angle) position-scaling drive and its Cartesian log-Jacobian, used by the nonequilibrium replica-exchange move (RENE).

A scale factor s displaces the scaled bond/angle coordinates of every eligible body about a shared anchor mu, moving each body's subtree as one rigid assembly so that its own (r,theta) relative to its parent become \( (s r - (s-1)\mu_r,\; s\theta - (s-1)\mu_\theta) \). Only Slider/Cylinder (bond length r) and BendStretch/SphericalCoords (r and bond angle theta) expose a scaled DOF; all other joints are left unmoved. The map and its log-Jacobian read the identical (r,theta) geometry from the z-matrix, so they stay mutually consistent (INV-7).

Note
This module is the drive and Jacobian only; it performs no acceptance, no work accumulation, and no run-type dispatch.
