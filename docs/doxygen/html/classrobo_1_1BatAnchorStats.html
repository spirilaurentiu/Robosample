Detailed Description

Accumulator for the shared, state-independent BAT scaling anchor mu that applyBatScaling requires (INV-7).

Holds one running mean per scaled BAT coordinate (keyed by the scaled body's zI atom index, the key applyBatScaling reads), updated from equilibrium samples across all thermodynamic states. The anchor SHALL be a single value shared by both partners of a swap pair and frozen across their forward and reverse drives; otherwise the paired map is not an exact involution. Owned by Context, not per thermodynamic state.
