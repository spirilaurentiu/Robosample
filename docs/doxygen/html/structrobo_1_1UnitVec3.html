Detailed Description

A unit-length 3-vector, normalized at construction.

Note
Constructing from a vector shorter than 1e-300 yields the fallback direction (0,0,1) rather than a NaN. Converts implicitly to Vec3 (a UnitVec3 is-a direction), so it may be passed anywhere a Vec3 is expected.
