Detailed Description

Body mass properties: mass, mass center com, and UnitInertia (inertia per unit mass) about the mass center.

Constructing from a full Inertia divides by mass to store the unit inertia (and yields a zero tensor for a massless body). reexpress rotates the properties into a new frame (com' = ~R*com, unit inertia re-expressed); toSpatialInertia converts to the 6x6 SpatialInertia operator. This is the storage/description type; SpatialInertia and Inertia are the operator and full-tensor forms (OQ-4, see findings).
