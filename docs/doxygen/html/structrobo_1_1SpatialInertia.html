Detailed Description

Rigid-body spatial inertia about the body origin, as mass, mass center offset com (origin->COM), and UnitInertia about the origin.

Acts as the 6x6 spatial mass operator: operator*(SpatialVec) maps a spatial velocity to spatial momentum (block form [[m*G, m*crossMat(com)],[~., m*I]], delegated to ArticulatedInertia for a single source of truth). Distinct from MassProperties and Inertia; see the OQ-4 note in the findings.
